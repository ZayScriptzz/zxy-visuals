About

Immersion is the only standard.

ZXY Visuals is a Montreal-based visual production company built on one principle: your audience shouldn't just see it — they should feel like they were there.

We combine aerial drone, immersive FPV fly-throughs, and cinematic ground cinematography into a single seamless visual language. From real estate listings to brand content, music videos to live events — every frame is built to move.
